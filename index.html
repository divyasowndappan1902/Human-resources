CONTACT SUPPORT

What specific HR services do you provide?
We offer a comprehensive suite of HR services including talent acquisition, employee onboarding, performance management, payroll processing, benefits administration, and HR compliance consulting. Our solutions can be tailored to fit the specific needs of your organization, whether you are a small startup or a large enterprise.

How does your pricing model work?
Our pricing is flexible and designed to scale with your business. We offer tiered subscription plans based on the size of your workforce and the specific features you require. Additionally, we provide custom pricing packages for enterprise clients with complex needs. Contact our sales team for a detailed quote.

Can you integrate with our existing software?
Absolutely. Our platform is built with connectivity in mind. We offer seamless integrations with popular payroll systems, applicant tracking systems (ATS), time-tracking tools, and communication platforms like Slack and Microsoft Teams. We also provide a robust API for custom integrations.

How long does the implementation process take?
Implementation timelines vary depending on the complexity of your requirements and the size of your organization. For most small to mid-sized businesses, standard implementation can be completed within 2 to 4 weeks. Enterprise deployments may take 6 to 12 weeks. We assign a dedicated onboarding specialist to guide you through every step.

Do you provide ongoing support after setup?
Yes! Customer success is our top priority. All plans include standard email and chat support. Premium and Enterprise plans also include dedicated account managers, priority phone support, and regular strategic review sessions to ensure you are getting the most out of our services.
